What do the axes measure?
What does the financial data mean?

Bubble size follows the selected financial figure on a log scale: every tenfold step adds the same width, so a $10B company reads one step larger than a $1B one, not ten times larger. The scale is fixed across filters. Post-money valuation and cumulative funding are separate measures. Hollow circles mark an unknown figure, one that has not been disclosed, not zero. Figures of $10M or less are drawn at the minimum size, never smaller than a hollow mark. Exact values are in each tooltip, the list and every profile.

Estimates and rumored figures say so wherever they appear. Every company profile says whether a figure was reported, estimated or entered manually; manual values have no public source. A funding total whose source has not been recorded yet says so on the company profile. Platform products have no standalone company valuation; their parent’s value is excluded. Acquisition prices and historical valuations are not interchangeable. An acquired company keeps its circle inside a thin ring; its figure is the acquisition price when that was disclosed, otherwise its last private valuation, and its hover card says which.

What is included in this map?

Focus categories are editorial groupings based on the descriptions in this dataset. Some products span several groups; each appears once under its primary focus. The dataset also retains adjacent and acquired products for comparison.

The news feed lists published coverage only. Pages visited to verify a fact are kept separately, in each profile’s verification log.

What do the axes measure?
What does the financial data mean?

Bubble area is proportional to the selected financial figure on a fixed scale across filters. Post-money valuation and cumulative funding are separate measures. Hollow circles indicate an undisclosed figure, not zero.

Reported values, sourced estimates, and manual values are labeled wherever a valuation appears. Manual values have no public source. Platform products have no standalone company valuation; their parent’s value is excluded. Acquisition prices and historical valuations are not interchangeable.

What is included in this map?

Focus categories are editorial groupings based on the descriptions in this dataset. Some products span several groups; each appears once under its primary focus. The dataset also retains adjacent and acquired products for comparison.
